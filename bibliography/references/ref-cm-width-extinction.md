# [CM08] Colding–Minicozzi, Width and finite extinction time of Ricci…

Tobias H. Colding and William P. Minicozzi II. *Width and finite extinction time of Ricci flow*. Geom. Topol. **12** (2008), no. 5, 2537–2586 (arXiv:0707.0108). The expanded companion to the JAMS note ([[ref-coldingminicozzi]]): the same width–extinction argument, but with the harmonic-map min-max machinery the note black-boxes now proved in full — §3 constructs the energy-decreasing map, §4 shows bubble convergence implies varifold convergence (the approximation property of the min-max), §§5–7 complete the propositions and the energy–area equivalence. This is the source that graduates the min-max proposition from debt.
