# [Bam07] Bamler, Ricci flow with surgery

Richard H. Bamler. *Ricci flow with surgery*. Diplomarbeit, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München (advisor: Bernhard Leeb), 2007. Available from the author's page. A compact (~100 pp.) self-contained route to the surgery construction: compactness theorems, no local collapsing via ${\mathcal L}$-geometry, classification of $\kappa$-solutions, the canonical neighborhood theorem, and the construction of a Ricci flow with surgery with justification of the canonical neighborhood assumptions — a fourth perspective next to the book-length expositions, often with cleaner statements.
