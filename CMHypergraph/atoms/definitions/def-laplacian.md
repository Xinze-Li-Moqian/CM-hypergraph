# Definition — Laplacian

## Statement

> [!definition] Laplacian
> On a Riemannian manifold, the Laplacian of a smooth function is the divergence of its [[def-gradient|gradient]]:
> $$
> \triangle f \;=\; \operatorname{div}(\nabla f) ;
> $$
> in Euclidean space $\triangle f = \sum_i \partial_i^2 f$. With this sign the heat equation reads $\partial_t u = \triangle u$.
