# Definition — free product

## Statement

> [!definition] Free product
> $G * H$: the group of alternating words in the nontrivial elements of $G$ and $H$, with juxtaposition as multiplication and no relations between the factors. Given homomorphisms $K \to G$ and $K \to H$, the amalgamated product $G *_{K} H$ is $G * H$ modulo the relations identifying the two images of each $k \in K$.
