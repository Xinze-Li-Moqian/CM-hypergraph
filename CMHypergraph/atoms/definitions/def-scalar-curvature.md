# Definition — scalar curvature

One number per point: the volume of a small ball falls short of (or exceeds) its Euclidean value at a rate measured by $R$.

## Statement

> [!definition] Scalar curvature
> The *scalar curvature* $R$ of $(M, g)$ is the $g$-[[def-trace|trace]] of the [[def-ricci-curvature|Ricci curvature]].

## Notes

> [!note]- Notes (click to expand)
> - **Picture.**
>   ![[scalar-ball.svg|633]]
> - Quantitatively: $\operatorname{Vol} B(p, r) = \omega_n r^n \big( 1 - \tfrac{R(p)}{6(n+2)} r^2 + O(r^4) \big)$ — positive $R$ means small balls are smaller than Euclidean.
