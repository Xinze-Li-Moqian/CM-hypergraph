# Definition — connected sum

## Statement

> [!definition] Connected sum
> For connected $n$-manifolds $M_1$, $M_2$: remove the interior of an [[def-embedding|embedded]] closed $n$-ball from each and glue the resulting boundary spheres by a [[def-diffeomorphism|diffeomorphism]]; the result is $M_1 \,\#\, M_2$.
