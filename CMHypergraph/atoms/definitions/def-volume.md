# Definition — volume

## Statement

> [!definition] Volume
> For a Riemannian manifold $(M, g)$, $\operatorname{Vol}$ denotes the measure of the Riemannian volume density of $g$; $\operatorname{Vol} B(x, r)$ is the volume of the [[def-riemannian-distance|metric ball]] of radius $r$.
