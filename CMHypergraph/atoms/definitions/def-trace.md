# Definition — trace

The total stretching of a linear map — the basis-independent sum of its
diagonal.

## Statement

> [!definition] Trace
> For an endomorphism $A$ of a finite-dimensional vector space, $\operatorname{tr} A$ is the sum of the diagonal entries in any basis (basis-independent). For a bilinear form $b$ on an inner-product space, the *$g$-trace* is $\operatorname{tr}_g b = \sum_i b(e_i, e_i)$ over any orthonormal basis $\{e_i\}$.
