# Definition — Ricci curvature

The average of the curvature over all planes containing one direction —
the quantity that controls the volume growth of geodesic balls, and the
one the Ricci flow moves by.

## Statement

> [!definition] Ricci curvature
> The *Ricci curvature* of $(M, g)$ is the [[def-trace|trace]] of the [[def-curvature-tensor|curvature tensor]]:
> $$
> \operatorname{Ric}(X, Y) \;=\; \operatorname{tr}\big( Z \mapsto \operatorname{Rm}(Z, X) Y \big),
> $$
> a symmetric bilinear form on $TM$; $|\operatorname{Ric}|$ is its $g$-norm.
