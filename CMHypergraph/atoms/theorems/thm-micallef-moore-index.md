# Theorem — Micallef–Moore: the min-max index bound

One unstable direction, no more: the minimal spheres a one-parameter
min-max produces have index at most one — the sweep supplies the single
direction of instability, and there is no room for a second.

## Statement

> [!theorem] Min-max index bound
> A branched [[def-conformal-map|conformal]] [[def-minimal-surface|minimal]] $2$-sphere obtained as a limit of a one-parameter min-max over a nontrivial [[def-sweepout-width|sweepout]] family has [[def-minimal-surface-index|index]] at most one: the Morse index is bounded by the number of parameters of the family.

## Notes

> [!note]- Notes (click to expand)
> - The principle is mountain-pass: a critical point produced by minimizing the maximum over a one-parameter family has Morse index at most the number of parameters. Micallef–Moore established this for the harmonic-map / minimal-sphere min-max, where CM's Proposition (existence) states the index bound but attributes its proof to this external result.
> - Consumed by [[prop-minmax-minimal-spheres]] and [[thm-almost-minimizing-compactness]] (the "index at most one" clause).
> - Blackbox for this graph — a node, not proved.
